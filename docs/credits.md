# Base and credits

**Community game base: [RenoDX DLSS5 / RHI repository](https://github.com/RankFTW/rhi-repo)**
— [downloads](https://github.com/RankFTW/rhi-repo/releases).
Our game measurements used [RenoDX DLSS5 4.70](https://github.com/RankFTW/rhi-repo/releases/tag/renodx-dlss5-4.70),
[ReShade 6.8.0 with addon support](https://reshade.me/) and the community NR 310.8 runtime.

**Laboratory foundation: [kibblerz/DLSS5-Reshade-AIO](https://github.com/kibblerz/DLSS5-Reshade-AIO)**
and its DLSS5-Feeder dependency provided the standalone measurement environment.
The recorded research checkouts are `74b9a2d4b32dcf958833ef11e712c153accada9d`
and `75754b2278914d4b4bfb0accc4fd3d6333369b42`, respectively.
AIO is the laboratory foundation; the installable adapter currently targets the
RenoDX DLSS5 integration named above.

**Model/runtime:** [NVIDIA DLSS](https://github.com/NVIDIA/DLSS).

**External performance reference:** [TechSpot's DLSS5 measurements](https://www.techspot.com/article/3170-real-dlss-5-performance/)
provide the attributed RTX 5070 Ti values.

MIT covers this project's original files. Third-party components retain their own
terms; [package contents](distribution.md) identifies the distributed material.
